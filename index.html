One little keep. Endless waves.

A cozy idle defense game for Android. You don't build a maze of towers: you protect one small keep, train the defenders on its walls, and climb further every time you start over.

What you'll do

Simple to start, with a lot to grow into.

Hold the keep

Waves come from every side. Fall, and your keep repairs and fights on a few waves back.

Train defenders

20 defenders from four factions: Humans, Elves, Orcs and Undead, each role playing differently.

Hatch pets

20 pets to hatch and evolve to five stars, each with its own skills and a partner defender.

Rise again

Ascend, be crowned and found a Dynasty. Every new start makes your keep stronger.

A look inside

How a run plays

Defend

Your defenders fight on their own. Spend the coins they earn to level them up while the waves keep coming.

Train

When your keep hits a wall, spend gold in the Garrison, hatch pets and collect relics to push further.

Ascend

Start over with crowns for upgrades that last forever, and watch the next climb go faster.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free to play. Ads only show when you choose to watch one for a reward, and the one optional purchase, "Ad-free rewards", gives those rewards without watching.

Do I need internet?

No, you can play offline. Ads and cloud save need a connection when you use them.

Will my progress be saved?

Progress is saved on your phone, and with Google Play Games sign-in it is also saved in the cloud, so you can continue on a new phone.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. The Little Keep is coming to Android first.

Which languages?

English and Dutch.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

In the game, go to Settings, then Send feedback, and paste the report into an email to berenpret@gmail.com.
